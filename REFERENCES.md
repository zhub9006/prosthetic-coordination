# Clinical Trials — Affordable Prosthetic Solutions

## Summary of Relevant Clinical Trials

### 1. NCT07437664 — AI-Powered Robotic Hand (3D-Printed, Low-Cost)
- **Title:** A Computer-Aided Design Approach for Customized Soft Transradial Prosthetic Sockets Using 3D Scanning
- **Sponsor:** Al-Nahrain University
- **Status:** Active Not Recruiting
- **Focus:** Low-cost, AI-powered, 3D-printed prosthetic hand using EMG signals and AI for adaptive grasping
- **Location:** Baghdad, Iraq
- **Keywords:** Low-cost Prosthetics, 3D Printing, AI, EMG, Adaptive Grasping

### 2. NCT04059107 — 3D Printed Myoelectric Prosthetics for Children
- **Title:** Evaluation of 3D Printed Myoelectric Prosthetics in Children With Upper Congenital Limb Deficiency
- **Sponsor:** Oregon Health & Science University / Limbitless Solutions
- **Status:** Enrolling by Invitation
- **Focus:** 3D-printed myoelectric prosthetic devices for children with congenital upper limb deficiency
- **Locations:** Portland, OR; Orlando, FL
- **Keywords:** 3D Printed, Myoelectric Prosthetics, Children, Congenital

### 3. NCT06560567 — 3D Printer Adoption in Prosthetics
- **Title:** Attitudes and Barriers of Prosthetics and Orthotics Professionals in Turkey Towards 3D Printers and Technology
- **Sponsor:** Medipol University
- **Status:** Completed
- **Focus:** Survey of prosthetics professionals on 3D printer cost barriers and adoption
- **Location:** Istanbul, Turkey
- **Keywords:** 3D Printing, Cost Barriers, Prosthetics, Technology Acceptance

### 4. NCT05048355 — Powered vs. Passive Prosthetic Knees
- **Title:** Metabolic Cost of Walking With Passive vs. Powered Microprocessor Controlled Knees
- **Sponsor:** Össur Iceland ehf
- **Status:** Completed
- **Focus:** Evaluating cost-effectiveness of powered prosthetic knees vs. passive microprocessor-controlled knees
- **Locations:** Reykjavik, Iceland; Bayreuth, Germany
- **Keywords:** Powered Prosthetics, Microprocessor Knees, Metabolic Cost

### 5. NCT04354493 — Myoelectric Training and Outcomes
- **Title:** Myoelectric Game Training and Outcomes
- **Sponsor:** Liberating Technologies, Inc.
- **Status:** Completed
- **Focus:** Evaluating myoelectric training tools for prosthetic outcomes
- **Location:** Holliston, MA
- **Keywords:** Myoelectric, Training, Upper Limb, Prosthetics

### 6. NCT04035928 — 3D Digital Scanning for Maxillofacial Prosthetics
- **Title:** Digital Design for Maxillofacial Prosthetics
- **Sponsor:** University of Michigan
- **Status:** Withdrawn
- **Focus:** 3D scanning for faster prosthetic fabrication (reducing cost and time)
- **Location:** Ann Arbor, MI
- **Keywords:** 3D Scanning, Maxillofacial Prosthetics, Digital Design

### 7. NCT05376332 — Sonomyographic Upper Limb Prosthetics
- **Title:** Sonomyographic Upper Limb Prosthetics: A New Paradigm
- **Sponsor:** George Mason University / Infinite Biomedical Technologies
- **Status:** Enrolling by Invitation
- **Focus:** Using ultrasound-based sonomyography for improved prosthetic control
- **Location:** Fairfax, VA
- **Keywords:** Sonomyography, Prosthetic Control, Upper Limb

## Key Trends
- 3D printing and AI are increasingly used to reduce prosthetic costs
- Myoelectric and sonomyographic control systems improve functionality
- Low-cost, 3D-printed solutions are emerging as viable alternatives to traditional prosthetics
