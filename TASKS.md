# Prosthetic Outreach Initiative — Volunteer Task Coordination

## Overview
This project coordinates volunteer tasks for the prosthetic outreach initiative, focused on finding and implementing affordable prosthetic solutions.

## Teams & Tasks

### Clinical Research Team
- [ ] Identify and review relevant clinical trials for affordable prosthetic solutions
- [ ] Compile findings into a research summary
- [ ] Contact research leads for collaboration opportunities

### Outreach Team
- [ ] Coordinate outreach events across Chicago, Boston, and Atlanta
- [ ] Schedule meetings and coordinate logistics
- [ ] Track volunteer assignments and availability

### Partnerships Team
- [ ] Identify potential partners (hospitals, NGOs, universities)
- [ ] Reach out to clinical trial sponsors and researchers
- [ ] Organize volunteer training sessions

### Operations Team
- [ ] Set up communication channels (Slack, email lists)
- [ ] Manage project documentation and resources
- [ ] Track milestones and deadlines
